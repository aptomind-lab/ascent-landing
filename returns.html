Returns and refunds

What we can change, what we put right, and what we cannot take back.

Before your order ships

Write to us and we will change or cancel it. Nothing is final until a parcel is handed over for dispatch, so this is the quickest way to fix an address, a size or a quantity.

If something arrives damaged, incorrect or short

We replace it or refund it, whichever you prefer. Send us your order reference and a photograph of the vial and the packaging within 5 days of delivery and we will arrange it. Transit damage and picking errors are ours to put right, and you will not be asked to pay return shipping on them.

Everything else is final once shipped

These are laboratory compounds, shipped lyophilized in sealed vials. Once a vial has left our control we have no way to establish how it was stored or handled, so it cannot go back into stock and be supplied to anyone else. That applies whatever the reason for the return, including a change of mind, and it is why we would rather answer questions before an order than after it.

Colombia: your right of withdrawal

Purchases made in Colombia carry the right of withdrawal set out in Ley 1480 de 2011, the Estatuto del Consumidor. You have five business days from delivery to withdraw from a distance purchase and return the product in the condition it arrived in, sealed and unopened. Return shipping is the buyer’s. This right sits above the paragraph before it and we honour it in full.

How to start

Message us on WhatsApp with your order reference. We answer in Spanish and English, and it is the fastest route to a person who can look your order up.

How refunds are paid

A refund goes back through Wompi to the card or account the order was paid from. We do not send refunds anywhere else. Wompi settles in Colombian pesos, so if your order was quoted in dollars the amount that reaches you is set by your own bank’s conversion on the day, which is usually within a few days of our sending it.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when the policy changes. The version on this page is the one that applies to an order placed on the day you read it.
