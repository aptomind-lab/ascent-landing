Reconstitution

One line of math.

Peptides ship as lyophilized powder. Reconstitution is controlled dilution: concentration is vial contents divided by water added, and everything else follows from that. Our team can walk you through any batch protocol.

Lyophilized peptides keep refrigerated between 2°C and 8°C, dry and protected from light.
